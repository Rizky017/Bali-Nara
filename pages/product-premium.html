Herbal Incense

Ingredient-based fragrance · No post-production fragrance dipping

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Fragrance from the ingredients
within the formulation.

Incense made by blending selected aromatic ingredients directly into the incense mixture before shaping and drying.

Rather than dipping finished sticks in fragrance, the scent comes from the ingredients incorporated into the formulation.
