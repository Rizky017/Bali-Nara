BEHIND THE PRODUCTION

A Closer Look at Incense Making

Explore the stages involved in preparing incense products, from product requirements to packaging and order preparation.

Understanding how an incense order is prepared can help business buyers communicate their requirements clearly and plan their sourcing.

1. Product Requirements

Every project begins with understanding the buyer's requirements. Product type, preferred fragrance, dimensions, packaging, labeling, and intended market can all be discussed before specifications are confirmed.

2. Production and Preparation

Production arrangements depend on the selected product and the supplier's available capabilities. Buyers should confirm the actual process, product specifications, and quality requirements with the supplier before placing an order.

3. Quality and Packaging

Product inspection and suitable packaging are important considerations when preparing an order. Packaging requirements should be agreed upon in advance, especially for private label projects and international shipments.
